# Time picker

`mud-time-input` is a time field: type the time, or pick an hour and a minute from a list that
opens below. `mud-time-picker` is that list on its own. Both work on their own with `[(value)]`,
with reactive and template-driven forms, and with signal forms.

```ts
import { MudInlineMessage } from 'ngx-mud-ui/inline-message';
import { MudLabel } from 'ngx-mud-ui/label';
import { MudTimeInput, MudTimePicker } from 'ngx-mud-ui/time-picker';
```

## Basic

The value is a 24-hour `HH:MM` string such as `'09:30'`, or `null`; never a `Date`, since a time of
day has no date or time zone.

Typing takes digits only; the field adds the `:` itself. A first hour digit from 3 to 9 becomes
`03`–`09`, and a first minute digit from 6 to 9 becomes `06`–`09`. The time is committed as soon as
it's complete and allowed. On blur, an incomplete or refused entry goes back to the last time the
field held; clearing it yourself stays.

`placeholder` is the pattern shown in the empty field and behind the caret, such as `OO:MM` for
Romanian; it must keep the `HH:MM` shape, or it falls back to `HH:MM` with a warning in development.
`clearable` adds a × button that emits `cleared`.

## Limits

`min` and `max` are inclusive `HH:MM` limits; a malformed one is ignored. `minuteStep` (`1`, `5`,
`10`, `15` or `30`) shows and accepts only those minutes. `timeFilter` returns `true` for the times
that can be picked; an hour with no allowed minute is disabled. It runs for every hour and minute,
so keep it cheap. None of these add a validation error to your form.

## States

`variant` (`default`, `warning`, `success`, `destructive`), `invalid`, `required`, `readonly` and
`disabled` work as in a [Text input](?path=/docs/components-form-text-input--docs). `size` is
`md` (40px, default) or `lg` (48px).

## The picker on its own

The clock button or <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>↓</kbd> opens the picker and moves focus into it. Pick an
hour, then a minute: the minute completes the time and closes the picker. `<mud-time-picker>` is
the same list on its own; its `value` is set once both are picked. It takes the same `min`, `max`,
`minuteStep` and `timeFilter`, and `appearance="flat"` removes its shadow.

Bind `formControl` and bind `invalid` yourself, or bind `[formField]` and let the field drive

## Accessibility

- In the picker: <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> move in a column, <kbd>Home</kbd> <kbd>End</kbd> go to
  the first or last value, <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> switch between hours and minutes,
  <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> pick, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes and returns focus to the
  field.
- All texts are in the `timePicker` labels, translatable (see
  [Localization](?path=/docs/foundations-localization--docs)).
- Messages and the format hint are linked with `aria-describedby`; messages sit in a `role="alert"`
  region.

### Functions and types

- `parseTime(value)`: `{ hours, minutes }` or `null`; `formatTime(hours, minutes)`: `HH:MM`;
  `isValidTime(value)`.
- `isSelectable(time, constraints)`: whether a time passes `min`, `max`, `minuteStep` and the filter.
- `minutesForStep(step)`: the minutes a step allows; `MUD_MINUTE_STEPS`; `MUD_TIME_PATTERN`
  (`'HH:MM'`).
- `MudTimeValue`: `string | null`
- `MudMinuteStep`: `1 | 5 | 10 | 15 | 30`
- `MudTimeFilter`: `(time: string) => boolean`
- `MudTimeInputSize`: `'md' | 'lg'`
- `MudTimeInputVariant`: `'default' | 'warning' | 'destructive' | 'success'`
- `MudTimePickerAppearance`: `'elevated' | 'flat'`
