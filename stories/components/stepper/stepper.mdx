# Stepper

Guides the user through a flow in steps, such as a long application form. The rail shows every step
and its state; the selected step's content shows below it. Built on the CDK stepper.
`mud-step-indicator` is a static list of stages, without navigation.

```ts
import { MudIndicatorStep, MudStep, MudStepIndicator, MudStepper } from 'ngx-mud-ui/stepper';
```

## Basic

Put a `<mud-step>` per step in `<mud-stepper>`, each with a `label` and optional `supportingText`.
Its content is the step's content. `selectedIndex` is two-way bindable, and `selectionChange` emits
the previous and new step. Through a template reference, call `next()`, `previous()` and `reset()`.

<Canvas of={StepperStories.Basic} />

## Step states

Each step shows one of five states, worked out by the stepper:

- **current**: the selected step.
- **completed**: a visited step whose `stepControl` is valid, with a check mark.
- **blocked**: a visited step whose `stepControl` is invalid, with an exclamation mark. Its
  `errorMessage` replaces the supporting text.
- **available**: a step the user can jump to (without `linear`).
- **incomplete**: a step the user can't reach yet (with `linear`).

Set `state` on a step to choose the state yourself, or use the CDK's `completed` and `hasError`.

<Canvas of={StepperStories.States} />

## With reactive forms

Give each step a `stepControl` (a `FormGroup` or `FormControl`) and its state follows the form. Add
`linear` to stop the user moving past an invalid step: `next()` and clicks on later headers are
refused until the current step is valid. `linear` has no effect on steps without a `stepControl`.

## Confirming a step change

`stepClick` fires when the user activates a header (click, <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd>),
before the step changes. Call `preventDefault()` on it to stay, for example when there are unsaved
changes. Headers the user can't reach don't fire it.

<Canvas of={StepperStories.StepClick} />

## Vertical and compact

`orientation="vertical"` stacks the steps, with each step's content right under its header. A
horizontal stepper narrower than 600px switches to a dot rail without numbers; labels are hidden
but still read by screen readers. It measures its own width, not the window. `compact` forces the
dot rail at any width.

<Canvas of={StepperStories.Layout} />

## Step indicator

`<mud-step-indicator>` lists the stages of a process on an information page. Each
`<mud-indicator-step>` takes a `heading`, and its content (text or a button) goes under it. Steps are
numbered in order, so put them directly in the indicator, without a wrapper.

<Canvas of={StepperStories.Indicator} />

## Accessibility

- Each header is named by its `label` and `supportingText`; `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` on the
  step replace that. The rail is a list, so screen readers announce "step 2 of 4".
- The selected header has `aria-current="step"`. Completed and blocked steps add ", completed" or
  ", has errors" to their name (translatable in the `stepper` labels, see
  [Localization](?path=/docs/foundations-localization--docs)).
- Steps the user can't reach with `linear` are `aria-disabled`.
- The rail is one <kbd>Tab</kbd> stop. <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> (or <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd>),
  <kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd> move between headers; <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd>
  selects one.
- Each step's content is a region named by its header. Keep labels short: on the horizontal rail
  they don't wrap.

## API

### MudStepper

Selector: `mud-stepper`

<ArgTypes of={StepperStories} />

Inherited from the CDK's `CdkStepper`:

- `linear` (input, `boolean`, default `false`): requires each step to be valid before moving on.
- `selectedIndex` (input, `number`): the selected step. Two-way bindable.
- `orientation` (input, `'horizontal' | 'vertical'`, default `'horizontal'`)
- `selectionChange` (output, `StepperSelectionEvent`): `{ selectedIndex, previouslySelectedIndex,
selectedStep, previouslySelectedStep }`.
- `next()`, `previous()`, `reset()`: move between steps from code.

### MudStep

Selector: `mud-step`

<ArgTypes of={MudStep} />

Inherited from the CDK's `CdkStep`:

- `label` (`string`): the step's name.
- `stepControl` (`AbstractControl` or signal-forms field): the step's state follows its validity.
- `errorMessage` (`string`): shown when the step is blocked.
- `state` (`MudStepState`): sets the state yourself.
- `completed`, `hasError`, `editable`, `optional` (`boolean`)
- `aria-label`, `aria-labelledby` (`string`)

### MudStepIndicator and MudIndicatorStep

Selectors: `mud-step-indicator`, `mud-indicator-step`

<ArgTypes of={MudIndicatorStep} />

### Types

- `MudStepState`: `'current' | 'completed' | 'blocked' | 'available' | 'incomplete'`
- `MudStepperStepClick`: `{ index: number; step: MudStep; defaultPrevented: boolean; preventDefault(): void }`
